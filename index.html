COUNTING THE ERRORS

Ballot Error Rates in the 2025 Philippine Midterms

This study analyzes ballot error rates in the 2025 Philippine Midterm Elections using statistical and data-driven methods. By examining precinct-level data across regions, ballot types, and voter populations, the research aims to identify patterns that affect election accuracy and transparency.

Aligned with SDG 16 — Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions

Project Overview

This research investigates ballot error rates across Philippine regions in the 2025 midterm elections. The study evaluates whether ballot complexity and precinct size influence voting accuracy and explores potential systemic factors affecting ballot errors.

Research Goal

Identify patterns and predictors of ballot errors to improve election transparency and reliability.

Dataset Size

92,822 precinct-level records from the Philippine Midterm Elections dataset.

Methods

Statistical testing, comparative analysis, and exploratory data analysis.

Dataset

The dataset contains precinct-level electoral data including:

  • Registered voters
  • Actual voters
  • Overvotes
  • Undervotes
  • Valid votes
  • Vote counts per candidate

Source: Vianney Tumbagahan (2025) – Figshare Dataset

Research Questions

Are ballot error rates significantly different across Philippine regions?
Are error rates higher in multi-candidate senatorial ballots compared to party-list ballots?
Does precinct voter population influence ballot error rates?

Research Hypotheses

  • Ballot error rates vary significantly across regions.
  • Senatorial ballots produce higher error rates.
  • Precinct size significantly affects voting errors.
