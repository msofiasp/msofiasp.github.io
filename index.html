Ballot Blunders

A Statistical Analysis of Ballot Error Rates in the 2025 Philippine Midterm Elections

Understanding voting errors to support transparent and reliable elections.

Overview

Background

Ballot errors such as overvotes and undervotes can affect election accuracy and may reflect issues in ballot design or voter understanding. Understanding these patterns helps improve electoral integrity.

Research Questions

  • Do ballot error rates vary across regions?
  • Are senatorial ballots more error-prone?
  • Does precinct voter size affect error rates?

Proposed Solution

Apply statistical analysis on precinct-level data to identify patterns and factors influencing ballot errors.

Hypothesis

  • Regions show different error rates
  • Senatorial ballots have higher errors
  • Precinct size affects error rates

Data Collection

Dataset Overview

The project uses precinct-level election datasets containing information on registered voters, actual voters, overvotes, undervotes, and candidate results.

After removing precincts with zero voters, the final dataset contains 92,745 precinct records.
